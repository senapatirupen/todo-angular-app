Formula Used
EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ / ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

Where:
 P = Loan amount
 r = Monthly interest rate (Annual % / 12 / 100)
 n = Tenure in months (Years × 12)

We then simulate month-by-month to split EMI into
interest + principal and track principal repaid
in the first 5 and 10 years.
