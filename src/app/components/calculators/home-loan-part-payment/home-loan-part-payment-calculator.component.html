How This Works
EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ / ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

Where:
 P = Outstanding principal
 r = Monthly interest rate (Annual % / 12 / 100)
 n = Remaining months

Every year:
 - Normal EMI runs every month.
 - At the end of each year, extra part payment reduces principal.
 - EMI stays same, so tenure automatically reduces.
We simulate month-by-month to calculate total interest, new tenure, and interest saved due to yearly part payments.
