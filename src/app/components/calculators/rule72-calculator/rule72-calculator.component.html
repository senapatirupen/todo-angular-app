Rule of 72
Years to Double  ≈  72 / Annual Return (%)
Return (%)       ≈  72 / Years to Double
The Rule of 72 is an approximation to quickly estimate how long it takes to double your money at a fixed annual rate (or what rate is required to double in a given time).
