Formula Used (Inflation Projection)
Future Annual Expense:
  Eₙ = E₀ × (1 + i)ⁿ

Total with inflation:
  TotalInflation = E₀ × ((1 + i)ⁿ − 1) / i

Total without inflation:
  TotalFlat = E₀ × n

Where:
  E₀ = Current annual expense
  i  = Inflation rate (in decimal)
  n  = Number of years
This projects how your yearly living cost grows with inflation and how much extra you pay in total compared to a no-inflation world.
