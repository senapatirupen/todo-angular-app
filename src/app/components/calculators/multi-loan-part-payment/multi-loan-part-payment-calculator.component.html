How We Distribute (Pro Rata)
weightᵢ = principalᵢ × rateᵢ
shareᵢ  = (weightᵢ / Σ weight) × lumpSum

Then for each loan:
 - Calculate EMI from current principal, rate, tenure.
 - Simulate loan once without part payment (baseline).
 - Simulate again with one-time part payment in month 1
   (EMI unchanged, tenure reduces).
 - Interest saved = baseline interest − new interest.
 - Tenure reduced = baseline months − new months.
