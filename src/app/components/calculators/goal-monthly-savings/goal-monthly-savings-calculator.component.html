Formula Used (Goal-based Monthly Saving)
FV = P × ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1) / r

⇒ P = FV × r / ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

Where:
 FV = Goal amount
 P  = Monthly investment
 r  = Monthly rate (Annual % / 12 / 100)
 n  = Total months (Years × 12)
We assume you invest at the end of each month (like a standard SIP), at a fixed annual return converted to monthly compounding.
